Midnight Vault

You are the crew's inside hand. Tonight the vault is yours — if you can get in, lift the loot and reach the getaway van before the guards, the cameras and the clock catch up with you.

Steal the target amount on each floor, then reach the green EXIT.
Guards and cameras have vision cones. Get spotted and the alert rises — at 100% the alarm sounds and every guard hunts you. Get grabbed and you lose a life.
From Floor 3 on, laser grids sweep the corridors — cross only while the beam is dark. And guards hear running footsteps: sprint and they will come to investigate the noise. Sneak to stay silent.
